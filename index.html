Compare parts in shops

Pick a part type, then open any part in six Bulgarian shops to compare there.

Frame rates are estimates for 1080p at high settings (competitive games on low), not benchmarks, and real results can differ by 15% or more. Specs for processors and graphics cards come from the chip makers. Motherboards, memory, storage, power supplies and cases show typical specs for that class, because exact specs vary by model and brand. Shop links open a search for the part, so you see the current price there.
