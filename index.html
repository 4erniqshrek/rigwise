Pick your games. We pick the parts.

Choose the games you play and how fast you want them to run. For each game, Rigwise shows the exact parts that get you there, and the parts for the most fps possible, with rough ~ prices and links to Bulgarian shops.

Choose my games

Three levels

Low: 70–80 fps

Smooth and playable. The smallest parts that reach it.

Medium: 100–160 fps

Fast and fluid on a high-refresh screen.

High: 400+ fps

For competitive games. Heavy games can’t reach this, so we show the most the parts can do.

Which parts for my games?

Tap the games you play, then choose a level. Each game gets its own parts.

Compare parts in shops

Pick a part type, then open any part in six Bulgarian shops to compare there.

Frame rates are estimates at 1080p, not benchmarks. Prices marked ~ are rough estimates, not live prices. Shop links open a search for the part, so you see the current price there.
